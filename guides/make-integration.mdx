Notocat allows you to sync your contacts with Make.com. You can use our app to sync your contacts with Notocat.

## How to install the app

1. Go to [Make.com](https://www.make.com/) and sign in to your account
2. Access our [Make integration app](https://www.make.com/en/hq/app-invitation/4e69cd14363a023677810a6a21bae48f)
3. Click on the "Add app" button
4. Click on the "Install" button
5. Click on the "Finish" button

### Modules Overview

1. **Get Contacts** - Retrieves a list of contacts from your Notocat newsletter

2. **Sync Contacts with Notocat** - Create or update contacts in bulk (if the email already exists in Notocat, the contact will be updated)

### Usage Instructions

1. Set up your Make.com Scenario

* Add the **Get Contacts** module to retrieve your contacts list from Notocat's newsletter
* Add the **Sync Contacts with Notocat** module to push contacts from other sources to your Notocat newsletter

2. Authentication

Make sure to authenticate both your source application and Notocat within Make.com to allow data exchange.
For Notocat, you’ll need your API key from your account settings - [https://app.notocat.com/user/api](https://app.notocat.com/user/api)

3. Customize Your Sync

* You can map fields from your source contacts to match your Notocat fields (e.g., Name, Email, Custom Fields).
* Use filters or conditional logic within Make.com if you want to sync only specific contacts.

### Troubleshooting Tips

* Ensure your Notocat API key is valid
* Ensure the newsletter ID always starts with `wks_`
* If contacts are not syncing, check for errors in your Make.com scenario and ensure proper field mapping.
* Contact Notocat support if issues persist at hello@notocat.com
